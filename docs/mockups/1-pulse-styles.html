How should the album cover pulse on the beat?

Each preview runs on a fake beat (about 100 BPM) with a stand-in cover. In the real app, the beat and colours come from the song. Click the one that feels closest; you can pick more than one.

A. Pulse and glow

The cover thumps bigger on each kick, and a neon glow in its own colours blooms behind it. The screen-edge frame flashes too. Calm, clean, easy to watch for hours.

B. Glitch hit

On each beat the cover splits into red and blue copies and slices jump sideways, like the Instagram post. High energy; louder drops glitch harder.

C. Ring of bars

A circle of frequency bars dances around the cover, bass on one side and treble on the other, while the cover pulses gently. The classic "music visualiser" look.

These can also be mixed, for example A as the base look with B kicking in only on big drops.
